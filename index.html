The app

Built for matchday.

A quick look on Saturday morning, every goal on Saturday afternoon, the full story by the evening.

Today

Your weekend on one screen.

Favourite your teams and leagues. The home screen leads with what matters right now: your next match counting down, live games ticking along, fresh results and the hottest stories of the day.

  • Live ticker with pulsing live markers
  • Teams and leagues you follow come first
  • "Today in focus" strip with the day's top stories
Match center

Every goal, every corner, every card.

Quarter-by-quarter scores, a timeline of goals, penalty corners and cards, full shootout detail, line-ups, head-to-head and the table as it stood on the day.

  • Penalty shootouts attempt by attempt
  • Line-ups with captains and keepers
  • Head-to-head record between the clubs
Tables

Tables that move with the game.

Standings update as results come in, with arrows for every place gained or lost. Each league gets its own zones, so you see at a glance who is heading for the playoffs and who is fighting relegation.

Playoffs & promotion Playdowns Relegation
Tournaments

From pool stage to final.

Knockout brackets for the German championship finals, the Euro Hockey League and the FIH Hockey World Cup. Two-stage pool formats with carried-over results, best-of-three series and every classification match.

  • Brackets fill in as the rounds are played
  • Pools, second-round groups and placings
News

The story behind the score.

Match reports, weekend roundups and previews, generated from the match data within hours of the final whistle. Not just for the top flight: the lower leagues get their stories too.

  • Reports with scorers, comebacks and table context
  • Roundups and previews for every level
  • League and federation news in one feed
And more
